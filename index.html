Why You Shouldn't Be Afraid to Fly on Airplanes

Introduction


As most of you know, airplanes are a form of travel to reach different parts of the world. Let's say you are in your hometown and want to visit family across the world. There is only one way to get there, and it is through an airplane. It soars at around 600 mph (965 km/h) and is 40,000 feet (12,000 meters) above ground. For some people, this is nerve-racking and scary.


It is normal to feel this way. Don't let this fear prevent you from seeing your loved ones. To make sure this fear doesn't haunt you, I made some suggestions for you to take and to eventually overcome your fear.


1. Trust the Pilots


These people guide the plane to fly smoothly from point A to point B, and they are really good at keeping it smooth. Pilots are strictly trained and have harsh requirements to fly a commercial plane.


To make sure everyone has a safe flight, pilots are expected to have a private pilot license, FAA medical certificate, pass 3 FAA tests, training hours with an offical instructor, 100 hours in a powered plane, and 100 hours in a Pilot in Command training. Also to be a pilot, it is recomended to get a Bachelor of Science in Aviation Management. To get this degree you would need 121 hours in class per semester, 66 in general and core classes, and 55 in everything about flying a plane.


As we can see from the training and harsh requirements, pilots are trained so well they can make your flight a smooth ride from take off to landing. They will know what to do if there is something wrong, and they will fix it like no one else can. Therefore we can entrust our pilots so we can sit back and relax while we watch movies in our seat.


2. Chances of Plane Crash vs Chances of Car Crash


Let's start off with the chances of being in a fatal car crash. Overall, there is a 1 in 107 chance of being in a fatal car crash. This is a little less than a 1% chance to happen. On the other hand a fatal plane crash is 1 in 11 million. The percentage for this is 0.000009%! That is five whole zeros!


The chances of a fatal plane crash are so low compared to a fatal car crash. Even though most people drive on the roads everyday, most of them make it out alive with a 1 in 107 chance. Just imagine riding a few plane rides in your lifetime, it is basically just comming out alive every single time. Dying in a plane crash is around the same chance of being struck by lightning twice in a row!


Overall, if you can ride a car safely everyday, you can almost gaurentee a safe plane ride.


3. Worst Case Scenarios Turn To Happy Ending


Something bad that could happen is the engine giving out and failing mid-flight. The engine powers the entire plane and without it, there could be some real problems. Engines are below the wings and they allow the plane to propell forward. When it fails the plane slows down little by little.


The plane is designed to slow down little by little to maximize the amount of airtime the plane could glide for. If both engines go out, the plane could glide for 60 miles. This allows the pilots to make emergency landings in runways in a 60 mile radius.


For that reason, even if you enter the worst case scenario, you will be able to make it out safely. Making out safley is everyones happy ending.


4. Surviving A Plane Crash


If you've been on a plane before, you know the flight attendants always make a speech about safety instructions and procedures during takeoff. This speech is to ensure that everyone knows what to do when there is an emergency. Most of us don't listen to it, but it is very important that we do.


This is because in case of an emergency we know what to do and we can have a better chance of making it out alive. Emergency exits, seat belts, how to use oxygen masks, flotation devices, and many other things, are things that are explained so we know what to do during an emergency. This gives us a higher chance at surviving and a way that everyone won't get hurt after the crash. Also, flight attendants make sure everyone is safe to make sure no one is left behind or severly hurt.


So even if you do get into a plane crash, there are many steps into ensuring everyones safety.


5. Best Seat On The Plane


Most people who are scared of flying, might be afraid of sudden turbulence in the aircraft.Turbulence is when the plane feels like it is shaking or unsteady. Turbulence happens because of an imbalance in the air from things like weather and terrain. People are scared of this because it feels as if the plane will stop flying. But there is a way to fix it. If you are scared of this sensation, you can do one thing.


Before picking your seat on the airplane, you should try getting a seat closer to the front or near the wings of the plane. The front is usually a good place to sit because the back of the plane usually experiences more turbulence compared to the front. For the seats near the wings, they experience less turbulence because the wings keep the plane balanced and smooth.


To make sure you have a smoother flight, be sure to remember this tip to reduce the amount of turbulence you will experience. This will prevent stress when turbulence does occur.


Conclusion


As we can see, flying isn't so bad. Even if there are a few ways that things can go wrong, they will eventually lead to something favorable. Pilots can be trusted, practically imposible to die in a plane crash, even without an engine the plane could land well, surviving a plane crash is easy too, and there are some awesome seats to sit at to reduce turbulence.


So now you can finally enjoy that trip to your loved ones. Maybe even enjoy the flight to your desination. Safe travels!
